The relay forwards F1 live timing. Car positions need your F1 TV token on the relay.

How to get your F1 TV token

Do this on a computer in Chrome. The Quest browser has no developer tools.

  1. Go to f1tv.formula1.com and sign in with your F1 TV Pro account.
  2. Press F12 (Mac: Cmd + Option + I) to open DevTools.
  3. Open the Application tab. If you can't see it, click » at the top of DevTools.
  4. In the left column, expand Storage → Cookies and click each formula1.com entry until you find a cookie named login-session. Typing it into the filter box helps.
  5. Click that row and copy the whole Cookie Value shown at the bottom. It's long; select all of it.
  6. In the Cloudflare dashboard, open Workers & Pages → kerbside → Settings → Variables and Secrets.
  7. Edit the F1TV_TOKEN secret, paste the value, and click Deploy.
  8. Press Connect here. The line above shows when the token expires.

Repeat these steps when live positions stop and the relay says the token was refused. These steps are based on how F1's site worked when this was written and may change.
