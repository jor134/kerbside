# Driver avatar model

`driver.glb` is made from **"rhp_bell_hp77_driver_f1"** by **MattDoesBlender**
(https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/rhp-bell-hp77-driver-f1-2916ac047ea84abe9394c38e4fa2d4bd),
licensed under **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/).

Changes (tools/driver_model.py): the face and eyes inside the helmet, the clear covers, tear-offs, visor decal and
stickers, logo patch and tag were removed; the mesh was cleaned of duplicate faces (about 52k triangles); small parts were baked to
vertex colours; the suit, gloves and helmet are tinted per person in the app; and the model was rigged with 15 bones so
it can be posed. This adapted model is shared under the same licence (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0): non-commercial use only.
