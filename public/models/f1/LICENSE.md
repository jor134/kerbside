# Race car

`car.glb`: "Ferrari F1 2019" by valvetin (https://sketchfab.com/valvetin), https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/ferrari-f1-2019-1b050bffe4e749b586b4782ee7ff4fd0,
licensed CC BY 4.0 (http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes: all textures and every sponsor/brand decal removed, cockpit details dropped, parts recoloured to solid groups
(paint, carbon, metal, tyre, rim) and the mesh simplified with quadric edge collapses to about 50k triangles (tools/hq_car.py with tools/qem.c).
`car_mini.glb` is a lighter copy made the same way (about 20k triangles) for the small cars on the table. Both are drawn from both sides and smooth-shaded with hard edges kept.
In the app the paint takes each team's colour and the rest is drawn dark.

Ferrari is a trademark of Ferrari S.p.A. No names or logos are used; the shape is still recognisably the 2019 car.
